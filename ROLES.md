# Team Roles

## Member 1 — Literature Review and Research Framing
- Review research on mammography-based cancer detection and model robustness to image-quality changes.
- Summarize relevant studies, datasets, methods, evaluation metrics, and limitations.
- Help define the research question, objectives, and study design.
- Draft the Introduction and Related Work sections.
- Coordinate the paper outline and help review the full manuscript.

## Member 2 — Dataset and Preprocessing
- Confirm dataset access, labels, permitted use, and relevant documentation.
- Organize the images and labels and check for missing or inconsistent data.
- Prepare preprocessing steps and patient-level training, validation, and test splits.
- Document the dataset and preprocessing procedures.
- Do not upload restricted mammography images to this repository.

## Member 3 — Model Development and Experiments
- Select and implement the agreed deep-learning models.
- Document the training setup and experiment settings.
- Apply the agreed image-quality variations at specified levels.
- Keep code and experiment configurations organized and reproducible.
- Draft the model and experimental setup sections.

## Member 4 — Evaluation and Reporting
- Evaluate models on original and altered test images using the agreed metrics.
- Create tables and figures comparing the results.
- Analyze performance changes and relevant errors.
- Draft the Results section and contribute to the Discussion and limitations.
- Help prepare the final report and presentation.

## Shared Responsibilities
- Agree on the dataset, prediction task, models, transformations, and metrics before experiments begin.
- Review the methods and results together.
- Keep decisions and experiments documented in the repository.
- All members contribute to reviewing and approving the final paper.
